My brother and I have a shared interest in strawberry hydroponics and robotics. And we had this idea to grow strawberries year-round in 40-feet shipping containers. So, we set up a vertical farming club at Berkeley in the summer of 2023, right before I started high school. We named our one-arm strawberry farmer 'Watney', from a movie called *The Martian*, because we dreamed of growing strawberries on Mars someday.
